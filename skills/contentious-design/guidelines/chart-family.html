what more than three series becomes
More than four categories at once is a chart-type decision, not a colour one. There is no categorical palette past --comp-4, and thirteen hues cannot be told apart whichever families they come from — so past four, encode the category by position (small multiples, a heatmap, rows) and leave colour to the value. Every ring takes one band ratio. --ring-band is 0.16 of the outer diameter — innerRadius = 1 - 2 × band, so the donut is 0.68 — and a gauge and a donut on one page are then the same weight of mark. The gauge shipped at 0.07 and read as a third of it. A label has one position, not two. A name that moves in and out of the fill depending on the value gives a set of rows a ragged left edge and a rule to work out, so a label that cannot always fit inside sits above the bar instead — one position, legible at any length. The time axis is the full window and the data starts where it starts: nothing before the first reading, no flat run at zero, and empty periods in a grid drawn as --limestone-700 cells.
