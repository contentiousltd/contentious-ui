Three of these were never spacing settings. The 56px under Estate is Watchlist's fifth line: cards stretch to the tallest, so unequal copy lengths show as dead space. Four columns at 276px wraps a paragraph to three words a line, which is why four across is conditional on one-line copy rather than free. And the two sets' illustrations carried different amounts of baked-in whitespace, so equal CSS gaps still looked unequal: the 6u box sets the optical size, and artwork is exported trimmed to its ink bounds. All four homepage illustrations were trimmed on 4 August. estate looked trimmed and was not: it carried a full-canvas near-white wash at about 8% alpha, which read as a pale rectangle behind the houses on the limestone card ground.

Two things came back from implementation and are now the rule, not deviations. The ground is --surface-raised, because these blocks sit on the page rather than holding data and limestone-300 read as too faint on a limestone ground. And body copy holds at --t-body-app while art, titles and gaps step up: at the 24px base a paragraph reaches 26px and dominates the card. Hover, where the card is a link, is .c-feature--link: the illustration grows 7% on --motion-breathe, 2400ms, and the title takes --accent-link on --motion-state. No lift and no shadow, which would put a second surface depth on the page. Five other candidates were weighed in explorations/Feature card hover.html. Hover the cards below.

Section rhythm, added 4 August. Removing the tinted band that used to inset "How this works" also removed the padding the band was carrying, and nothing replaced it: the live homepage has 58px between the card row and the next section's heading against 40px of padding inside every card, so "Choose your content health plan" reads as part of the row above it. .c-marketing-section gives a section 2u top and bottom, so adjacent sections sit 4u apart. The rule is a ratio and therefore checkable: space between sections is at least three times the largest gap inside one. A section owns its own vertical space and the page never adds margins between them; --tint puts a band back on the same geometry. The name is not .c-section: the app already owns that class as a top-margin spacer.
