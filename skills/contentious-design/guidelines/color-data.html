Muted is darker than zero, and that is the whole ordering: the more meaningful the figure, the darker it is, with a genuine nothing the quietest of the three. Muted is for a secondary figure in a cell that already has a primary – it is not a way to calm a busy screen, and reaching for zero to de-emphasise a live number says the number is nothing. Never accent-by-default. Rendering every value in fire – including $0.0000 and 0% – spends the alarm colour on nothing and leaves no way to signal a real problem.
