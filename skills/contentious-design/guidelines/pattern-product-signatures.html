Rules the layer enforces

  • A product overrides tokens/products.css only. No product hex anywhere else, in any repo.
  • The overridable set is closed at 29 tokens. A 30th is a system decision. It has grown three times, every time because a shipping product wouldn’t fit — most recently --surface-hover, because a hover step is not the raised band.
  • No two products share the same (deployment, ground, accent) triple. CM and CHC — sold in one conversation — must differ on ground.
  • A partial signature silently inherits CHC. Set all 29 or none.
  • Banded is the studio site's alone. A second banded product dilutes the one thing marking the parent as the parent.
  • Chrome and fields read --text-inset, never --text-body — VTS runs a light bar over a dark page.
  • Diverge at the front door, converge inside: --signature-app-ground says which.

Open, and worth deciding soon

  • VTS's stop, not its family. Ground is sorbet — corrected. sorbet-850 is set here; sorbet-700 is lighter than the shipping page and reads as a card stop. Check the live CSS.
  • Accents are tight, the palette isn't. The 500-stop reservation binds only products that display scores — which is why the studio site may run full-bleed sunshine. Wave is the last free accent for scoring products.
  • CHC holds fire as accent and star-1. A fire button beside a fire one-star bar. Precedent, not licence.
  • Density is settled, not accidental. Derived from deployment: 19px every app, 24px marketing. 19 rather than CHC's 18 because 19 is the library's own pre-0.2.0 default — the base the component CSS was drawn against. Land it before chc-367.
  • --signature-display-scale is declared but --t-title and --t-metric don't multiply by it yet. Wire it before anything sets it — VTS's 1.18 is the only non-1 value.
  • Texture is studio-only. The app rule — no gradient, blur, glass or grain — stands. An app growing a torn edge is a defect, not a family trait.
